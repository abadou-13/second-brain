## Summary
To join a football club in Paris, one needs to research and choose a suitable club, check the club's requirements, and register with the French Football Federation (FFF). The process involves several steps, including getting a medical certificate and attending trials or training sessions. Popular clubs for amateur players include Paris Université Club, CA Paris, and US Créteil.

## Key Ideas
- Choose a football club in Paris that suits your interests, skill level, and location
- Check the club's specific requirements, such as age, skill level, and registration fees
- Register with the FFF (Fédération Française de Football)
- Obtain a medical certificate (certificat médical) to prove fitness for play
- Attend trials or training sessions to showcase skills
- Consider joining a local football association for players of all levels
